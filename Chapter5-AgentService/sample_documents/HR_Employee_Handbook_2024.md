## 2. Employment Policies

### 2.1 Equal Employment Opportunity
Contoso is an equal opportunity employer. We prohibit discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.

### 2.2 Employment Categories
- **Full-Time**: ≥30 hours/week, eligible for all benefits
- **Part-Time**: <30 hours/week, pro-rated benefits
- **Contractor**: Temporary engagement, no benefits
- **Intern**: Educational placement, stipend-based

### 2.3 Probationary Period
New employees undergo a 90-day probationary period. Performance is evaluated at 30, 60, and 90 days.

---

## 3. Time Off & Leave Policies

### 3.1 Vacation Time (Annual Leave)
| Tenure | Annual Vacation Days |
|--------|---------------------|
| 0-1 year | 15 days |
| 1-3 years | 18 days |
| 3-5 years | 20 days |
| 5+ years | 22 days |

**Accrual**: Vacation accrues monthly (1.25 days/month for first year).
**Carryover**: Maximum 5 days may be carried to next calendar year (requires manager approval).
**Payout**: Unused vacation is paid out upon separation per local law.

### 3.2 Company Holidays (10 days/year)
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day
- New Year's Eve

### 3.3 Sick Leave
- **Full-time**: 10 days/year (accrues at 0.83 days/month)
- **Part-time**: Pro-rated based on hours worked
- **Usage**: Personal illness, medical appointments, caring for immediate family
- **Documentation**: Required for absences >3 consecutive days

### 3.4 Parental Leave
- **Birth/Adoption**: 16 weeks paid at 100% salary
- **Foster Care**: 8 weeks paid at 100% salary
- **Eligibility**: 12 months continuous service
- **Job Protection**: Position guaranteed for return

### 3.5 Bereavement Leave
- **Immediate Family**: 5 days paid (spouse, child, parent, sibling, grandparent)
- **Extended Family**: 3 days paid (in-law, aunt/uncle, cousin)
- **Flexible**: Additional unpaid time available with manager approval

## 4. Workplace Policies

### 4.1 Work Hours & Flexibility
- **Core Hours**: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM local time (mandatory availability)
- **Flexible Start**: 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM
- **Flexible End**: 3:00 PM - 6:00 PM
- **Remote Work**: Up to 3 days/week with manager approval (hybrid model)
- **Full Remote**: By exception only (VP approval required)

### 4.2 Code of Conduct
All employees must:
- Treat colleagues with respect and professionalism
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Report ethical concerns via Ethics Hotline: ethics@contoso.com

### 4.3 Dress Code
**Business Casual** in office. Client-facing meetings: business professional. No offensive graphics, beachwear, or athletic wear (except company-branded).

---

## 5. Compensation & Benefits

### 5.1 Pay Schedule
Bi-weekly (26 pay periods/year). Direct deposit required.

### 5.2 Health & Wellness
- **Medical**: 3 plan options (PPO, HMO, HDHP with HSA)
- **Dental**: Preventive 100%, Basic 80%, Major 50%
- **Vision**: Annual exam + frames/lenses/contacts allowance
- **Mental Health**: 10 free therapy sessions/year via Lyra
- **Wellness Stipend**: $500/year for fitness, apps, equipment

### 5.3 Retirement
- **401(k) Match**: 100% of first 4% + 50% of next 2% (max 5%)
- **Vesting**: Immediate
- **Roth Option**: Available

### 5.4 Additional Benefits
- **Education Reimbursement**: $5,000/year (pre-approval required)
- **Commuter Benefits**: Pre-tax transit/parking up to IRS limits
- **Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)**: 15% discount, lookback feature
- **Life Insurance**: 2x salary (company-paid), optional supplemental

---

## 6. Performance & Development

### 6.1 Performance Reviews
- **Annual Cycle**: January (review) → February (calibration) → March (delivery)
- **Mid-Year Check-in**: July (informal, no rating)
- **Rating Scale**: Exceeds / Meets / Developing / Needs Improvement

### 6.2 Career Development
- **Individual Development Plans (IDPs)**: Required annually
- **Mentorship Program**: Opt-in, 6-month cycles
- **Internal Mobility**: Open roles posted internally first (2 weeks)

---

## 7. Separation Policies

### 7.1 Voluntary Resignation
- **Notice**: 2 weeks minimum (4 weeks for Director+)
- **Exit Interview**: Mandatory with HR
- **Final Pay**: Next regular payday (includes accrued vacation)

### 7.2 Involuntary Termination
- **Performance**: Progressive discipline (PIP → final warning → termination)
- **Conduct**: Immediate for gross misconduct
- **Severance**: 2 weeks/year of service (min 4, max 26 weeks)
